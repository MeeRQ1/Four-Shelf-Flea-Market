How to play

  1. Buy a find. Each item needs a free shelf. Tags tell you which buyers want it.
  2. Choose a buyer. Matching items light up. Select the requested number from your shelves, then sell them to collect the offer. A sale frees shelf space immediately.
  3. Plan ahead. Check a find arriving tomorrow. You can hold items overnight or clear a shelf for half the item’s cost, rounded down.
  4. Close the stall. Stock refreshes and offers due that day expire. After day seven, leftover finds sell at clearance prices and your cash becomes your score.
